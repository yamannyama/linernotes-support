Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 14, 2026
Aves Inc. · 日本語

Liner Notes has no accounts. What you record stays on your iPhone.

Your diary, automatic records, conversation records, and voice and face features are stored on your iPhone. The developer (Aves Inc.) has no system for storing the content of your diary or conversations on its servers. To write text or create pictures, the app may send the text it needs to the route you choose (Apple's Private Cloud Compute, or OpenAI). Sections 4 and 5 describe exactly what is sent and when.

This page describes, as concretely as we can, what the app handles and where. By starting to use the app, you agree to this policy.

1. What stays on your iPhone

The following is stored on your iPhone:

  • Diary entries, notes, letters, and the day's pictures
  • Automatic records (places you stayed and their coordinates, photo times and notes about their content, summaries of sleep, steps, exercise and heart rate, and calendar events)
  • Conversation records (transcripts, speakers, and summaries)
  • Voice features (of your voice and of people you have named; these are numbers used to tell voices apart, not audio)
  • Face features (numbers used to recognize people in your photos; not the photos themselves)
  • Body and voice signs (records of how your heart rate and voice differed from usual)

Deleting the app removes these from your iPhone. If you made a copy (Section 6), you can restore from it.

2. Information read with your permission, and why

Every permission is optional. Anything you don't allow simply isn't used, and the app still works.

Location (Always)

We use iOS visit detection to record where you spent time each day. It does not track your route continuously. Place names are looked up with Apple's services. To show the day's weather, your location is passed to Apple's weather service (WeatherKit).

Photos

Photos taken that day are added to your diary, and objects, text and people in them are recognized on your iPhone (Apple Vision). Photos themselves are never sent off your iPhone. Photos stored in iCloud Photos may be downloaded from Apple for display.

Health (HealthKit)

We read sleep, steps, exercise and workouts, active and dietary energy, time in daylight, mindful minutes, resting heart rate, heart rate, and heart rate variability (SDNN and RMSSD) to build the day's record and to show when your body was tense or relaxed. When you record a conversation on Apple Watch, the app uses a workout session to keep recording, but it never saves a workout. HealthKit data is never used for advertising or marketing, or shared with third parties.

Calendar

Event titles, times, and attendee names are used as material for your record. The app writes an event only when you choose to add one.

Microphone and speech recognition

Used when you speak a diary entry or note, or record a conversation. Speech is converted to text on your iPhone (Section 3).

Music

Songs you listened to are added to your record, and songs are picked from your library's patterns. To look up song details, the song title and artist may be sent to Apple's search service.

3. Conversation records and Apple Watch

  • We ask before you first keep conversations. You can change this anytime in Settings.
  • A spoken note is kept as a conversation when it lasts 2 minutes or more and includes another person's voice.
  • Recordings are transcribed and split by speaker on your iPhone. Once transcribed, the recording is deleted.
  • Audio recorded on Apple Watch is sent to your iPhone and processed the same way. It is deleted from the Watch once your iPhone confirms receipt (and after 3 days at most if it never arrives).
  • Voice features of people you name are kept so their voices can be recognized. Voice features of people you haven't named are deleted within 90 days.
  • Summaries, titles and speaker guesses are made with Apple's Private Cloud Compute (Section 4). Audio is never sent.
  • Please tell the people around you when you record.

The speech models are downloaded from Hugging Face (a model distribution service) the first time you use them. None of your records are sent in that request.

4. Where text is created (AI route)

In Settings → AI route, you choose where text and pictures are created:

  • On this iPhone only — created with on-device Apple Intelligence.
  • Apple's private cloud — created with Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Available on iOS 27 and later.
  • Also use OpenAI — the OpenAI API is also used, under the conditions in Section 5.

When Apple's private cloud is used

For drafting and polishing your diary, the morning note, diaries from notes, conversation summaries and speaker guesses, and song picks, the text needed is sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute. This includes diary and note text, conversation transcripts, and a summary of the day's record (weather; numbers such as sleep, steps and resting heart rate; event titles and attendee names; place names; and notes about photo content). According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfill the request, is not stored, and is not accessible even to Apple.

5. When OpenAI is used

OpenAI is used only after you agree in the app, and only while you subscribe or during the Premium Preview (your first 3 mornings, up to 7 days, no card required, no automatic charge). You can turn it off anytime in Settings → AI route.

  • Recipient: OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States), via our relay server to the OpenAI API.
  • When: the day's picture, the weekly letter, finding songs, and organizing memories (checking whether two topics are the same). It is also used for drafts and the morning note when Apple's private cloud is unavailable, or when you choose "OpenAI (more natural)".
  • What is sent: diary and note text; conversation summaries (never the transcripts themselves); a summary of the day's record (weather; numbers such as sleep, steps and resting heart rate; event titles; place names; notes about photo content); and your library's patterns for song picks (frequent genres and artists).
  • What is not sent: audio, photos themselves, detailed health records (such as heart rate over time), and voice or face features.
  • Training: under OpenAI's API data usage policy, text sent is not used to train AI models. Retention follows OpenAI's API terms.
  • Relay server: only relays the request; it does not store or log the content.

6. Copies (backups) and export

  • iCloud Drive copy: if you choose it, a copy of your records is placed in your iCloud Drive every day.
  • Export: in Settings you can export all your records anytime as Markdown (zip) or as a full copy.
  • Deletion: you can delete records in the app. Deleting the app removes the records on your iPhone. We hold no copy.

7. No tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-collection tools. We don't measure your usage or share it with others.

8. Payments

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store). Payment details are managed by Apple; we never receive them. Only App Store purchase information is used to verify a subscription.

9. Age rating and legal compliance

The app is rated 4+. We comply with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and related laws and guidelines, and respect the privacy laws that apply in each country and region where the app is offered.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features are added or laws change. We'll announce important changes in the app or on this page. Continuing to use the app after an update means you agree to the updated policy.

11. Contact
