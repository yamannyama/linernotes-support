Questions

Do I really not have to write?

Really. A draft of your day is already there, built from places, photos, sleep, steps and plans. Read it, fix what you like, and keep it. On days you'd rather talk, one line into the mic is plenty.

Where are my records kept?

On your iPhone. There's no account, and your diary and conversations aren't stored on the developer's servers. You choose where text is created (on your iPhone, Apple's private cloud, or OpenAI) in Settings.

What happens to other people's voices in a conversation?

Recordings are turned into text on your iPhone and deleted afterward. Only the voice features of people you name are kept, to recognize who's speaking. Voice features of people you haven't named are deleted within 90 days. Whether to tell people in the room is up to you.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. With Apple Watch you can keep conversations from your wrist, and your day's timeline shows when your body was tense or relaxed.

I'm worried I'll quit halfway.

While you're away, the automatic record keeps going, and it's waiting for you the day you come back. There are no streaks, so there's nothing to lose.

What's the difference between free and the subscription?

Free gives you the daily diary, automatic records, drafting and polishing, and export forever, plus a morning note once a week. The subscription ($3.99/month, or $29.99/year with 7 days free) adds a morning note every day, weekly letters, conversation records, the day's picture and tonight's record. Your first 3 mornings (up to 7 days) are free to try with no card.
